# ScopePay AI Hackathon Demo Script

Before recording, prepare one Gemini API key, one PayPal Sandbox Business app, and a separate PayPal Sandbox Personal buyer account. Keep secrets out of screen recordings.

1. Open the public ScopePay AI website. Show that no ScopePay username or password is required.
2. Open Integrations and connect your own Gemini key. Do not show the key after pasting it.
3. Select a sample project or create a new project, enter a client request, and run a live AI analysis.
4. Review the evidence, effort estimate, and negotiation alternatives.
5. Publish options and open the generated client invitation link in another tab.
6. Accept an option or send a counteroffer, then show the freelancer-side resolution.
7. Connect your PayPal Sandbox merchant app in Integrations.
8. Open the payment link and use a separate Sandbox Personal buyer account.
9. Show the server-verified payment status and capture ID.
10. Optionally open an incognito window to demonstrate that it receives a separate workspace and cannot see the first visitor's projects.

Do not describe sample fixtures as live Gemini output. Do not use real PayPal credentials or real money.
